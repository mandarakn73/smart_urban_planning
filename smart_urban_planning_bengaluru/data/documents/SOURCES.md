# Planning knowledge sources

The local RAG indexes the planning documents and searchable text stored in this folder.

1. `RMP_2031_Volume_1_Vision_Document_Draft.pdf` — Revised Master Plan for Bengaluru 2031 Volume 1 draft. Treat as planning-background/draft material, not as final binding approval text.
2. `Bengaluru_Zonal_Regulations_Amendment_2025_Premium_FAR.pdf` — Government of Karnataka zonal-regulation amendment document containing premium FAR provisions. Verify jurisdiction and effective date before applying a rule to a particular parcel.
3. `Bengaluru_Zonal_Regulations_Amendment_2025_Text.md` — searchable text extraction of the above PDF for local RAG.
4. `Bengaluru_Greater_Bengaluru_Zonal_Regulations_Amendment_Search_Text.md` — searchable transcription/OCR-derived text from the scanned zonal-regulation material. Treat as a search aid and verify against the source document.
5. `Bengaluru_Zonal_Regulations_Scanned.pdf` — scanned zonal-regulation source; local RAG may rely on the searchable transcription for retrieval.
6. `Bengaluru_Zonal_Notification_Additional.pdf` — additional zonal notification source retained for research reference.

The application deliberately distinguishes observed GIS context, retrieved regulatory evidence, design heuristics and unsupported/unavailable data. Numeric planning values should not be interpreted as automatic legal approval for a site.
